What the garden shows

Every flower comes from a public comment on the YouTube channel Planted by Strangers. For each flower this page shows the name that was commented, the public YouTube handle of the person who commented, the day it was planted, the kind of flower and, if it was shown in the video, the reason. Nothing else from YouTube is kept here. Comments with links, advertising or unkind content are not planted.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing the garden the videos are about (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Removing or changing a flower

You can have a name, a handle or a whole flower removed or corrected at any time, without giving a reason. Write to plantedbystrangers@gmail.com or leave a comment on the channel. The same applies if someone else planted a flower with your name. Parents and guardians can ask on behalf of a child.

When you open this page

The page is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). To deliver it, Vercel's servers process the technical data your browser sends with every request, such as your IP address, the time, the address you opened and your browser type. This is necessary to show you the page (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Vercel works on our behalf; the data may be processed in the USA.

Cookies and storage

This page sets no cookies and does not use your browser's storage. Fonts and scripts come from this site, not from third parties.

Links to YouTube

Buttons such as “Plant a flower” and “Watch it bloom” open YouTube. Only then does YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd.) receive data from you; its own privacy policy applies.

Your rights
